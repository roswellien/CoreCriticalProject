Vulkan Graphics

The rendererGraphics optionsCurrent progressFollow the work

The original view, drawn by a modern GPU.

The game still decides what to draw. Core Critical’s Vulkan renderer takes those draw calls and presents them at your chosen resolution, window mode and aspect ratio, including ultrawide displays. The original menus keep their 4:3 layout, centered over a full-window HD background.

Every enhancement is optional. With them turned off, the renderer aims to match the original presentation, so improvements stay a deliberate choice.

Turn on what you want.

  • Resolution and displayWindowed, fullscreen or borderless play at modern resolutions, with ultrawide support, antialiasing and anisotropic filtering.
  • Lighting and shadowsMission sun and sky lighting, sun shadows, weapon and fixture lights, and mech headlights (L by default).
  • View distance and fogScenery distance from the original 800 up to 3200, with haze that scales to hide distant pop-in. Targeting and gameplay keep the original range.
  • Materials and packsUpgraded mechs, textures, normal maps and water and cloud detail from the content packs you enable, plus parallax depth on supported surfaces.

Playtesting across more hardware.

Playtest status · 27 November 2026

The renderer ships in every playtest build. Recent work covered GPU lighting, fog, loading, the menu presentation and recovery from some video memory failures.

More to check before release.

Longer view distances cost much more GPU time, and lighting, shadows and parallax still need checking across missions and graphics cards. Playtest reports guide that work.
